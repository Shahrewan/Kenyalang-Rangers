## Product and loop

- **FIXED**. Offline, single-player, landscape Android 2D tactical tower defense and conservation education, developed for Godot 4.7.2 and GDScript per the project configuration. The player is the friendly, faceless, androgynous Leader of the fictional Kenyalang Guardians. The audience is high-school age and older. Story combat, Headquarters management and four side activities share local progress. [GDD pp. 4–6 §§ B, C, F.1; p. 28 §§ N, O.1; repository README and AGENTS.md; user project configuration]
- **FIXED**. Inspect a mission or Chaos configuration, choose a loadout and eligible R&D effects, deploy to intercept sequential waves, then resolve the operation. Use earned Funds for Headquarters and permanent Ranger or Facility levels, and return to a replay or the next available operation. PR Outreach and Records run outside combat. [GDD pp. 4–5 §§ B, C.2–3; p. 14 §§ I.1–2]
- **FIXED**. The free demo has device-local progress and no accounts, network dependency, multiplayer, ads, purchases, gacha, subscriptions, telemetry or cloud saves. [GDD pp. 4–5 § C; p. 28 §§ N, O.1; p. 30 § R]

## Operation, waves and integrity

- **FIXED**. Each operation begins at **100 Operational Integrity**. Enemies follow marked paths to a tree-veiled forest tunnel. An ordinary combat escape subtracts its configured penalty. Integrity at zero is defeat. E15 Wildlife Trafficker escaping immediately defeats the mission. The Poacher Leader's escape is described as catastrophic. [GDD pp. 7–8 § G.1; pp. 12–13 §§ H.2–3; UI p. 36 S23]
- **FIXED**. Story clears when all active waves resolve and Integrity remains positive. A perfect Story clear requires **zero escaped combat enemies**. A non-escape loss of Integrity such as Heat preserves perfection if the operation still clears. The Chapter 1 cinematic messenger is outside the combat roster and never changes the escape counter. [GDD p. 6 § F.2; pp. 7–8 § G.1; p. 31 § S.3; UI pp. 8, 34–35 S21–S22]
- **FIXED**. Waves spawn enemies singly at configured intervals, with possible groups. A wave resolves when its enemies are defeated or have escaped. Rest occurs before the next populated wave. Boss-only waves are allowed. An empty Chaos slot adds neither spawns nor rest. [GDD p. 8 § G.1; pp. 21–22 § M.4; pp. 24–25 § M.7]
- **FIXED**. Pause and full-screen dialogue stop the shared combat clock. 1×/2× govern combat time for spawns, movement, attack and skill timers, cooldowns, construction, status effects and Heat. Dialogue offers Continue and Skip, preserving the same deterministic battle state on resume. Opening, selected wave transitions, boss introductions or HP thresholds, victory and defeat may trigger dialogue. [GDD p. 7 § F.4; p. 9 § G.5; pp. 29–30 §§ O.2–4, Q; UI pp. 20–24 S09/S09L/S09E, pp. 120–121 M07–M08]
- **PROVISIONAL**. GDD spawn/rest cadence is approximately 3.5 s/25 s for early C1, 2.5 s/22 s for late C1, 2.0 s/20 s for early C2, 1.5 s/18 s for mid C2 and 1.1 s/15 s for late C2. The documented wave totals are first-pass counts. Preserve those counts when tuning individual spawn groups for the same draft. [GDD pp. 21–22 § M.4; UI p. 8, Narrative Order]
- **MISSING**. Per-enemy ordinary escape penalties, detailed boss escape dispatch and phase scripting, exact spawn queues, path speeds and timing constants are `TBD`. [GDD pp. 7–8 § G.1; pp. 12–13 §§ H.2–3; pp. 22, 33 §§ M.4, T.4]

## Grid, placement and touch

- **FIXED**. Use original routes and separate ground and elevated cells. Ground Rangers serve as blockers or close combatants, with individual role exceptions such as K9 Ranger's tranquilizer rifle. Elevated Rangers attack eligible targets within square areas. Aerial enemies cannot be blocked on the ground. Ground melee attacks cover one or two tiles forward according to the Ranger. Choose **LEFT or RIGHT** during drag and lock that facing at deployment. The storyboard has Rangers face approaching enemies in its examples. [GDD p. 8 § G.2; pp. 9–11 §§ H.1–1a; p. 29 § Q; UI pp. 2, 25 S12, 119 M03]
- **FIXED**. Preview footprint, range and direction on drag, validate every occupied cell, and show a local reason on failure with no Token spend. The default Facility footprint is **two horizontally adjacent elevated cells**. **Sentry Tower** occupies one elevated cell and reserves the cell immediately above it. Its sprite is two tiles high. Barricade Ops and Strike Team use two ground tiles. Enemy route waypoints follow spawn → ground → tunnel; elevated cells are off the paths. [GDD p. 8 § G.2; p. 10 § H.1; pp. 15–17 §§ I.3, J.2; p. 32 § T.2; UI pp. 4, 25 S12, 66 S32, 99 S57]
- **FIXED**. A quick tap on release activates an eligible Ranger skill. Targeted skills arm and require target confirmation. Long-hold exposes Retreat near the Ranger and suppresses the tap action. Retreated Rangers can redeploy after cooldown, subject to named abilities. Unfinished Facilities can be cancelled for a full refund of paid Tokens. Built Facilities stay stationary and have no retreat action. [GDD pp. 8–9 § G.3; p. 15 § I.3; p. 17 § J.2; UI pp. 26–29 S13–S16]
- **PROVISIONAL**. 350 ms is the initial hold threshold. Sonic Turret has a documented fixed **7 s base build**. D10 can lengthen build time. Permanent levels preserve a Facility's base construction duration. Shared placement and hold tests on Android must be verified before locking touch tuning. [GDD pp. 8, 16–17 §§ G.3, J.1–2; p. 24 § M.6; UI pp. 27–28 S14–S15]
- **MISSING**. Exact target tie-breaks, square radii by unit, two-ground-cell orientation details and most build, cooldown and effect durations are `TBD`. [GDD pp. 8–11 §§ G.2–4, H.1–1a; pp. 15–17 §§ J.1–2]

## Logistic Tokens and the Starter Post

- **FIXED**. Logistic Tokens are transient. The normal opening begins at **0 Tokens** before applicable effects. Players may omit the Logistics Post from an ordinary Facility loadout. When equipped, the **first normal Post** is free, instant and bypasses the first cooldown. Later Posts have escalating costs, normal construction and normal cooldown. A Post's unmodified production is **10 Tokens per 10 s at Lv.1** and **15 per 10 s at Lv.100**, increasing between levels. [GDD p. 8 § G.3; p. 15 § J.1; p. 20 § M.2; UI pp. 2, 118 M02]
- **FIXED source intent.** **C1-01 supplies Scout Sniper, Patrolman and a Starter Post at the start**, even though fresh Headquarters opens without Mechanical. UI S10 shows two selected Rangers and the separately labeled **Starter Post**. Mechanical Lv.1 normally grants two Facility slots after a 20,000 Funds build. Preserve that tutorial access without treating two slots as built. [GDD pp. 9, 18–19, 22 §§ H.1, M.1, M.4; UI pp. 10, 22 S10, 39 S26, 62 S28]
- **MISSING implementation rule.** Starter allowance ownership, slot accounting, replay and end-of-tutorial lifetime and pre-Mechanical availability after C1-01 are `TBD` ([D-001 and D-009](DECISION_LOG.md#open-decisions)). A normal 0-Token setup with no Post can constrain deployment. Its exact legality and any valid alternative opener are `TBD` ([D-002](DECISION_LOG.md#open-decisions)). [GDD pp. 8–9, 19, 22 §§ G.3, H.1, M.1, M.4; UI pp. 10, 17 S06, 22 S10]
- **FIXED**. **D05 Fixed Stockpile** prohibits all Logistics Posts and starts at **120 fixed Tokens**. It overrides the first-free Post effect. C1-05 uses D05 and omits the free-Post tutorial. D01 reduces Post yield by 10%, including the C1-01 configuration. The 10/15 production endpoints are base values. See the [modifier catalog](SYSTEMS_INDEX.md#story-debuffs-and-chaos-modifiers) for D01, D05, D10 and B13 dependencies. **MISSING**. D05 plus B13 interaction, Token rounding and later Post costs are `TBD`. [GDD pp. 20, 22–24 §§ M.2, M.4–6; UI pp. 2, 22 S10, 101 S59]

## HP, Siege, statuses and Heat

- **FIXED**. The baseline Ranger and Facility HP floor is **100**. Damage removes HP after explicit named reductions or modifiers. There is no Defense or universal armor stat. Ordinary melee targets eligible ground Rangers. Ordinary ranged attacks may target ground or elevated Rangers. Only designated **Siege Damage** can damage Facilities, including construction sites. Demolitionist and Portable Mortar Crew are regular Siege threats. Ignition Operator's Heat acts on Integrity, independent of Facility HP. [GDD pp. 9–13 §§ G.4–5, H.1–3; p. 17 § J.2]
- **FIXED**. Slow, stun, immobilize, mark, reveal and Knockdown are source-specific. Named cover applies only while active. Combat Medic heals eligible allies. Outpost Mechanic repairs eligible Facilities and Barricade Ops. Underfoot HP bars cover Rangers, enemies and Facilities. Heat appears only on relevant stages. At full Heat, remove Integrity and reset the gauge. Firebreak Beacon slows accumulation and cannot eliminate it. [GDD pp. 9–11 §§ G.4–5, H.1–1a; pp. 16–17 §§ J.1–2; UI pp. 29–31 S16–S18]
- **PROVISIONAL**. Registry HP, hit damage, heal/repair amounts and attack intervals are starting figures. Worked baseline checks include a 160 HP Logistics Post surviving two unmodified 60-HP Siege hits and falling on the third, and a 320 HP Patrolman requiring 36 unmitigated 9-HP Poacher hits. **MISSING**. Heat rates, maximum, Integrity loss, status order, overlap and exact targetability constants are `TBD`. [GDD pp. 9–13 §§ G.4–5, H.1a, H.2a; pp. 16–17 § J.1a; p. 33 § T.4]

## Story progression

- **FIXED**. The Operations Manager is a young, capable planner who can be flustered. She forgets to recall a patrol from the old chokepoint, where the Rangers intercept a small poacher team. The supportive Leader helps her coordinate reinforcements. A cinematic messenger warns the larger operation. Chapter 1 ends with the Diversion Captain's capture, and Chapter 2 shifts to a more serious regional pursuit. [GDD pp. 6–7 §§ F.1–4; UI p. 8, Narrative Order]
- **FIXED**. Chapter 1 is **Forgotten Patrol**, five skippable tutorial missions on identical T01 geometry. Chapter 2 has ten missions, two per Gunung Mulu, Batang Ai, Tanjung Datu, Imbak Canyon and Sepilok-Kabili, in that order, on five original regional templates. Cleared missions replay. The previous node must clear even when Control reveals additional nodes from fog. There is one fixed debuff per C1 stage and two per C2 stage. [GDD pp. 5–8 §§ E, F.2–4, G.1; pp. 19, 21–22, 24, 32 §§ M.1, M.4, M.6, T.2; UI pp. 4, 8, 14 S03]
- **FIXED**. First-clear and perfect bonuses are separate one-time awards. Eligible perfect rewards can be earned alongside the first clear. Replays provide supplemental Funds. Story reward unlocks and the Firebreak Beacon provision are recorded in the [mission catalog](SYSTEMS_INDEX.md#campaign-missions). [GDD pp. 21–22 §§ M.3–4; UI pp. 34–37 S21–S24]
- **PROVISIONAL / MISSING**. Stage reward amounts pictured in the UI are illustrative. The per-stage first-clear, perfect and replay tables, including the final Story completion amount, are `TBD`. The PDF contains story beats and sample lines. Full Chapter 2 narrative, final lines and authored trigger data are external and `TBD` for ingestion. [GDD pp. 2, 7, 22, 33 §§ A, F.3–4, M.4, T.3–4; UI pp. 8, 10, 15 S04, 34–35 S21–S22, 98 S56]

## Headquarters and Funds

- **FIXED**. Headquarters has 11 modular rooms, Levels 1–5. Only Control Room, Human Resources and Finance Room start built at Lv.1, free. Eight rooms are constructed with Funds. Room builds and upgrades are instant. Control increases visibility, HR increases Ranger slots, and Mechanical increases Facility slots. Ecology adds plots and Outreach has a participation gate. The fixed room cost schedule totals **16,160,000 Funds**. [GDD pp. 18–20 § M.1; UI pp. 38–62 S25–H11/S28, 110 V08]
- **FIXED**. Rangers and Facilities advance from Lv.1 to Lv.100, with milestones at **Lv.20, 40, 60, 80 and 100** and exactly **1,000,000 Funds** baseline cumulative cost per deployable. Leveling improves existing raw outputs. Skills, geometry, charge limits and build times are retained. Records enhancements are separate. R&D levels only unlock distinct buffs; they never strengthen existing buffs. [GDD pp. 19–20 §§ M.1–2; p. 33 § T.4; UI pp. 64, 66–69 S30/S32–S34/F01]
- **FIXED**. Funds are the sole persistent spendable currency. A fixed held-wallet cap clamps incoming credits, with **permanent overflow disposal** and a visible warning. There is no overflow storage, compensation or alternate currency. Finance produces supplemental stored Funds only while active and unpaused, with its own room storage limit. Claim transfers eligible Funds to the same wallet. [GDD pp. 19, 21, 25, 28–29 §§ M.1, M.3, M.7, N, O.2; UI pp. 68 S34, 100 S58]
- **PROVISIONAL**. The starting balance is **10,000 Funds** and the demo cap example is **9,999,999 Funds**. The roughly **60-day** target covers all rooms and about **eight** preferred Lv.100 deployables, with 25.02M earned and 24.16M intended spend in the model. The cumulative level-price formula and room payout baselines are in the [economy contract](DATA_CONTRACTS.md#economy-and-progression). **MISSING**. Final numeric cap, balance behavior under a harmful response larger than the wallet, and final payout tuning are `TBD`. [GDD pp. 18–21, 26, 29, 33 §§ M.1–3, M.8, O.4, T.4; UI pp. 10, 39 S26, 100 S58]

## Rainforest Chaos

- **FIXED**. Choose an original route, configure up to **five** wave slots and **50 total enemies per populated wave** across all types, and use distinct eligible modifiers and unlocked compatible buffs. Empty slots skip. Sliders and plus/minus controls clamp immediately. The all-empty setup disables Start in the UI. Campaign limits buffs to one in Chapter 1 and two in Chapter 2. Chaos permits any number of different compatible unlocked buffs. Ineligible effects cannot contribute free score. [GDD pp. 14–15 §§ I.2–4; pp. 23–25 §§ M.5–7; UI pp. 72–74 S36–S38, 101 S59, 114 V12]
- **FIXED**. Score `S = Σ(enemy quantity × enemy points) + Σ(eligible debuff points) − 100 × filled Ranger slots − 100 × filled Facility slots − Σ(buff penalty magnitudes)`. Empty slots contribute zero. A run qualifies only if **every configured enemy is defeated and Integrity remains positive**. An escape prevents payout, trophy progress, unlocks and a personal-best update. [GDD pp. 24–25 § M.7; UI pp. 9, 74–76 S38–S40]
- **FIXED**. A nonpositive score or unqualified run pays zero. For qualifying runs, the exact piecewise-linear schedule is below. The wallet cap still applies. [GDD pp. 24–25 § M.7; UI pp. 9, 75–76 S39–S40]

| Score | Funds before wallet cap | First eligible threshold |
|---|---:|---|
| 1–999 | 20 × S | — |
| 1,000–1,999 | 40,000 + 40 × (S − 1,000) | 1,000, Skill Relay Station and Tier 1 |
| 2,000–2,999 | 130,000 + 70 × (S − 2,000) | 2,000, Specialist Calltower and Tier 2 |
| 3,000 and above | 300,000 + 120 × (S − 3,000) | 3,000, Sentry Tower and final Tier 3 look |

- **FIXED**. The trophy appears at the ground-floor HQ Chaos entrance and in Chaos. Its look stops changing at 3,000 and its personal-best number can exceed 3,000. The three threshold Facilities are Chaos-exclusive rewards. There are no share codes or online leaderboards. The worked reference computes **1,350 score and 54,000 Funds** from 1,550 enemy points, 400 debuff points, five filled slots and B05. [GDD p. 15 § I.4; pp. 24–25 § M.7; p. 30 § R; UI pp. 9, 71–76 S35–S40]
- **MISSING**. Final route capability matrix, ordered stacking and rounding of simultaneous modifiers, and handling of a first run that crosses multiple trophy thresholds at once are `TBD`. [GDD pp. 23–25 §§ M.5–7; UI pp. 73, 75, 101 S37/S39/S59]

## Local continuity and Android targets

- **FIXED**. One continuous local save loads automatically. Missing Leader name opens the naming screen. Settings is the sole entry to a confirmed wipe of progress and Leader name, which invalidates the prior progress backup. Use a versioned local save and last-known-good fallback. Save after stage completion, room/level purchase, PR response and Restoration collection. Idle PR/garden timing uses local timestamps. [GDD pp. 28–29 §§ O.1, O.3; UI pp. 12–13 S01–S02, 94–97 S52–S55]
- **FIXED**. Touch-first controls, landscape safe areas, scalable menu/dialogue text, distinct color plus shape/icon cues, audio sliders, visual equivalents, wave/enemy inspection and skippable tutorials/dialogue. Demo language is English. [GDD pp. 14–15 §§ I.1–3; p. 18 § L; pp. 28–29 §§ O.1, O.3–4, P; UI p. 3]
- **PROVISIONAL**. Compatibility renderer is preferred. Android 9, 3 GB RAM, ARM64/OpenGL ES 3, Android 11+/4 GB preferred and 60 FPS gameplay with approximately 60 FPS animation sampling are project targets. No device certification follows from these figures. Per-device baseline, performance thresholds and export-template verification are `TBD`. [GDD pp. 4, 28–29, 33 §§ C, O.1, O.4, Q, T.4; UI pp. 2–3]
