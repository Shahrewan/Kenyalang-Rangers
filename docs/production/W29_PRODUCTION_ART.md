## Delivery plan

Each row is a review package. Split larger rows into a board review, then one unit or related pair per import change. References and destination paths are keyed per asset in the [CSV](w29/asset_audit.csv). For Chapter 1, share the T01 field and use the exact stage IDs even when the art adapter is scoped to fewer stages.

**Standard-unit base rule.** Begin with the approved [Scout Sniper](../../assets/source-art/rangers/scout_sniper/manifest.json), [Patrolman](../../assets/source-art/rangers/patrolman/manifest.json) or [Forest Trespasser](../../assets/source-art/enemies/e01/manifest.json) source. Keep the existing head/body ratio, mouthless face, ink weight, soft earthy shading and feet, then change only the needed hair detail, clothing, equipment or accessory. Reference the same transparent textures when a part is unchanged. Show every standard variant beside its base at 80- and 60-pixel tile scale. Machines and companions use their required anatomy or chassis within the same line and color treatment. Reserve markedly different character silhouettes, proportions and costume masses for bosses.

**Common review sequence.**

1. **Inventory and reference.** Identify the live asset, master/source candidate or placeholder by stable key. Record version, exact destination, expected bounds and what can be sourced from the same layers. Pin the source page and approved live capture. Reference-only files stay out of player export.
2. **Visual review.** Compare a standard unit to its actual approved base at 80/60-unit tile scale and review UI at 1280×720 and 960×540. Check the exact base's proportions, ink weight and small-shape readability. Use familiar gear outlines or accents to signal the role. Review worst overlap and both mirrored poses. Get a dated owner decision per batch before reuse at scale.
3. **Art import.** Rerender layers against the master, check hashes and alpha padding, keep lossless import and actual sprite offsets and pivots. Connect joints in animated extrema and test 1×, 2×, pause, terminal cleanup and duplicate actor instances. Record tested asset names and clips.
4. **Integration and release.** Run exact stage/board and navigation regressions and compare existing gameplay outcomes before and after. Check packaged dependencies, fallback behavior and native captures. Review on a physical Android device with normal and enlarged text, safe insets, and the C23 load case in the relevant modes. Record D-012 hardware evidence and D-014 provenance, credit and permission for the exact shipped art. Keep unresolved content decisions in their existing approval flow.
