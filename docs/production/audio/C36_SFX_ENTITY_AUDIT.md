# C36-A · Combat entity audit

Reviewed production definitions against the W29 runtime identities. The planned mappings below are the same entries in `entity_coverage` in the JSON. Every Ranger and Facility also inherits the common field cues listed below.

- Rangers share accepted deploy, explicit facing when applicable, living retreat and zero-HP removal. Using a skill is separate from readiness. Shared melee profiles select their asset at the accepted action.
- Facilities share construction start, Ready, player-cancelled build and Siege destruction. The first instant Post skips construction audio. Passive Facilities share those lifecycle cues even if the Planned Binding cell reads **Lifecycle only**.
- Enemy groups share basic action clips where appropriate. A configured escape or real damage takes its identity from the battle registry. The Poacher Messenger is separately classified as noncombat.
- Shared damage and removal cues are optional for selected consequential actions. Positional rendering, continuous particle systems, status expiry and passive mechanics use visual feedback.
