## Patrolman brief

Second is the existing in-game Scout cutout artist's reference sheet, use its assembled left-side Scout figure as the battle-scale shape anchor, never copy the sheet layout or Scout's identity. Draw ONE standalone full-body Patrolman in neutral slight 3/4 view facing screen-right, feet on an even baseline. Use the reference's specific drawing language: enormous rounded-square warm-peach head and short brim together ~58% of standing height, compact jacket, very short squat legs and tiny simple stub hands, short dark hair in big locks, two round-ended short vertical black eyes with no pupils, NO MOUTH, no nose line. Flat opaque fills of olive #8E895E, sand #B5A17B, dark brown #5B4941, warm peach #F8C9A6 and warm ink #2B2622. Very thick smooth continuous warm-black outlines with rounded joins like the exact reference, restrained large shadow shape only, intentionally very few interior marks. Distinct costume from the Scout: short rounded olive cap, simple olive jacket, a narrow curved plain sand riot shield held close on far side of body and a short simple civil ranger training baton held down on the near open side. No firearm, scope, cape, animal, goggles, text, labels, scenery, tiles, shadow, white fringe, ground, or other characters. Very simple flat finished-edged game style like the reference, no detailed illustration, gradients, texture, cute shiny rendering, realism or anime. A single coherent silhouette readable at 68 logical px tall. Entire figure fully framed centered with generous clear margins. True isolated transparent alpha background. Keep this as a flattened silhouette review study, no exploded parts or additional frames.
