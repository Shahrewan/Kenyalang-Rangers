## Style lock

- **Characters.** Oversized heads and headgear occupy about 55–60% of standing height. Use short torsos and legs, broad compact boots, tiny hands, rounded-square faces, two short dark vertical eyes and mouthless tactical faces. Match the owner's shape language and W06's warm ink `#2B2622`, rounded joins and 2.2–2.4 logical unit outer stroke at Scout scale. For the approved Patrolman and E01 studies, retain the exact visible tones and shading in the cutouts. Use olive, oat, sand, bark and peach for compatible future artwork. Keep the same hair masses and costume silhouette on redraw. [W06](W06_RANGER_SPRITE_SPEC.md#on-field-style-and-neutral-poses) · UI pp. 2–3, 11, 22–23.
- **Read by silhouette first.** Scout retains the broad downturned marked brim, ponytail, mantle and scoped tranquilizer. Patrolman has a short rounded cap, narrow curved shield and small baton. E01 uses a squat plain outfit, bare tousled hair and a pouch. The owner approved cap removal for enemy artwork. Keep Ranger olive and use muted rust or brick-red accents to distinguish enemy clothing and gear. E01 is the first palette reference. Match the source lineup's chibi proportions without pulling its animals into First Contact. [W06 briefs](W06_RANGER_SPRITE_SPEC.md#twelve-ranger-art-briefs) · UI pp. 16, 19, 23.
- **Stage and UI.** Reuse the forest, olive, cream, sand, amber and rust system on UI p. 3. Put strong contrasts on actors and feedback. Keep the grid center quiet, with foliage on the outside. Use the fixed dialogue family for expressive upper-body portraits. The Leader is faceless and androgynous. Maintain an unobstructed text area. [Canon visual language](../canon/ART_AND_UI_SPEC.md#visual-language-and-screen-sizing) · UI pp. 3–5, 20–23.
- **Review scale.** Compose at the 1280 × 720 design reference and 80 logical unit maximum tile. Inspect branch screenshots at 1280 × 720 and 960 × 540 and on at least one named Android landscape phone. Record physical display size and scaled hit areas. W06 Ranger range is 62–78 units. Targets for this slice are Scout 72 (measured W06 height 72.157), Patrolman 68 and a proposed 66 for E01. Keep HP, highlights and props distinct at the smaller scale. [W06 cutout spec](W06_RANGER_SPRITE_SPEC.md#cutout-and-export-contract) · UI pp. 2–3, 23, 25.

## T01 art geometry

`T01` uses a 12 × 5 grid, entry at `(0,1)`, inclusive route `(0,1) → (4,1) → (4,3) → (11,3)` and a woodland escape at `(11,3)`. The 12 interior route cells are the ground placement set. The 19 raised tops and paired candidates follow the existing map, including `(1,0)+(2,0)` as a legal Post example and `(5,1)` as an isolated raised cell. Use the existing gate and facing data. Draw direction and occupancy from the same `grid_origin()`, `tile_size()`, `cell_center()` and footprint used by drag validation. [Map](../../data/stages/t01_forgotten_patrol.tres) · [Route](../../data/stages/t01_route.tres) · [Board projection](../../scripts/ui/sample_board.gd) · UI pp. 4, 6, 22–25.
